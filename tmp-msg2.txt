Pipeline: bound push CPU, and never lose liveness to it (NOT DEPLOYED)

The cron pipeline degraded from 60 cycles/hour to zero on 2026-10-09 and left
the site on "ДАНІ ЗАСТАРІЛІ". Diagnosis: dispatchPush ran BEFORE
recordChecksBatch, so an isolate killed mid-push (exceededCpu, per the existing
comment in push.js) left no liveness trace at all.

push.js
- MAX_PUSH_JOBS_PER_CYCLE = 24. `jobs` was unbounded: during a mass alert
  hundreds of new tracks were fanned out over every subscriber. Official
  alerts are sorted to the front BEFORE the cap, so a raid start/end is never
  what gets dropped; overflow is counted in `jobsDropped` and logged.
- Relevance misses (skipped-place / skipped-old) no longer write one D1 row
  per (subscriber x unrelated event). 2026-10-09 produced 9300 such rows, all
  batch-inserted in one statement every cycle. They now collapse into one
  aggregate row per reason per cycle.
- skipped-cap keeps its individual row: that is a deliberate decision and must
  stay auditable (existing test enforces this).
- firstSeenMap IN(...) capped 500 -> 32 placeholders.
- The per-cycle `DELETE FROM push_log WHERE ts < -30 days` is gone; the table
  is bounded by the aggregate rewrite and was never the bottleneck.

pipeline.js
- Monitoring liveness (NEPTUN/MAPA) is now recorded IMMEDIATELY after the
  monitoring lanes return, BEFORE any publish or push crypto. An isolate that
  dies later still leaves a check row, so "no data" can never again be silent.
- OFFICIAL is recorded separately once its lane settles.

backend/test/stability.test.js pins all of this:
- structural: checks are written before the first publish
- behavioural: 400 events x 3 subscribers stay bounded and are aggregated
- behavioural: a journal that throws on push_log cannot break the cycle
