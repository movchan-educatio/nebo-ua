Offline radar centre, full РАДАР.LIVE branding, filters survive a dead pipeline

Frontend only. No backend change in this commit.

Offline centre (no Nominatim dependency)
- services/ukraine-places.js: verified local directory of 34 places (27
  administrative centres + major cities). Each coordinate is checked offline
  against assets/data/ukraine-oblasts.geojson, so a wrong oblast pairing
  cannot ship.
- radar.js resolves the centre, popular chips, oblast select and the radar
  city labels from that directory FIRST. Nominatim is now only an optional
  enrichment for free text it does not know — it is never required to boot
  the scope, so a rate-limited or unreachable geocoder can no longer leave
  the radar without a centre.
- tools/v4-offline.mjs proves it: with nominatim AND the aggregator hard
  blocked, the radar still boots on Kyiv, a chip click switches to Kharkiv,
  and zero Nominatim requests are made.

Filters survive an outage
- The kind-filter panel was rendered only after a successful data load, so a
  dead pipeline left the left column empty exactly when the operator needs it.
  It now renders at boot.

Branding
- Every title, meta description, og:title, visible heading, the manifest and
  the service-worker push title now say РАДАР.LIVE instead of НЕБО.UA.
- tests/brand.test.js locks this in (old spellings, push titles, sitemap host).

Tests: 561 pass. Design V4, circular radar, filters, settings and mobile are
untouched and re-verified in the browser.
